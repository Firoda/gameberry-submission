Block Battle

1010!, head to head. Same board, same pieces, but now your clears hit back.

1Drag the 3 pieces onto your board. Fill a row or column to clear it.
2Clear 2+ lines in one move and a junk row rises on your opponent's board.
3Junk headed your way? You get 6 seconds: clear any line to block it.
4Three minutes. Junk can't knock you out, only running out of room can. Higher score wins. Every 200 points earns a round of applause.

In the real mode you'd be matched with a friend, a club-mate or a player at your level. A bot fills in when nobody is free.
