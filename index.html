1010! is Tetris that stopped falling. You drop three blocks on a 10×10 board, fill a row or column, and it clears. It is calm, endless and the original of the hottest genre in mobile. The only opponent it has ever given you is your own high score.

This deck proposes Block Battle: a 2-minute head-to-head mode where your clears land on your rival's board. The goal is +10% daily active players.

3The feature

Block Battle: same 1010!, but your clears become your rival's problem.

Play it vs a bot →
Same rules10×10, three pieces, rows and columns clear.
2+ lines = attackA junk row rises on your rival's board, with one gap.
Block itClear fast enough and incoming junk is cancelled.
2 minutesTop out and you lose; otherwise higher score wins.

Why it brings players back

  • Every clear has a target. A double clear is no longer just points; it is pressure on a person.
  • A loss is a rematch, not an ad. The end screen offers “Rematch: get revenge” and “Challenge a friend”.
  • Classic stays untouched. Battle is a separate mode with power-ups off, so skill decides.

Build it in three steps

1

Seeded duel (async). Both players get the same pieces; your recorded clears replay as junk on their board. No servers in real time.

2

Challenge link. “Beat me in Block Battle”; the link opens straight into the match, so it doubles as an invite.

3

Live 1v1 with skill-based matchmaking, once step 1 shows people come back for it.

Straight from Gameberry's playbook

Ludo STAR's loop is a quick match, a rematch and a friend invite. Gameberry reported that ~95% of its growth was organic, from invites built into the core game, and that 64% of weekly players swapped Lucky Dice with friends (2021). Block Battle puts that same loop inside 1010!.

Send-lines battles are proven in Tetris (Tetris 99) and exist in small block-puzzle apps (e.g. PVP Blocks, Block Clash); no major 10×10 game offers one. Gameberry figures: PocketGamer.biz, Aug 2021, self-reported.3 / 4

+11% is half of the outside benchmark: Duolingo, the language-learning app, found learners with a friend streak were 22% more likely to do their daily lesson (2024). It is halved because a match is lighter than a shared streak.

The % lift does not depend on 1010!'s real size; the model uses a 100K DAU index.

How we'd prove it

  • 90/10 holdout for 4 weeks. 10% never see Battle; matches form only inside the 90%, so the pool isn't halved.
  • Read next-day return in weeks 3–4, after the novelty fades. The base gap (1.8 pts) is ~3.5× the standard error of a single day's read.

Guardrails

Classic-mode return rate · ad revenue per DAU · crash rate · match wait time

Risks

Losing streaks → skill matchmaking and a bot fallback. Real-time cost → ship async first. Fewer ad views per session → watch revenue per DAU, not per session.
