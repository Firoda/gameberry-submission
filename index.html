Block BattleA 3-minute duel vs a friend's run; come back for revengeHighMedMedMedThe bet

Next: live 1v1 with matchmaking and a bot fallback. Later: Battle Clubs, where revenue comes in. Not doing: ad changes (off the table); freeze fixes are a must-do, not a growth lever.

How it works

  • Same 1010!, on today's board. Clear 2+ lines at once and a junk row with one gap rises on your rival's board.
  • The rival is a friend's recorded run, replayed on a board that takes your junk: if their next move no longer fits, they top out. No live servers needed.
  • 3 minutes; top out and you lose. Power-ups are off, so skill decides. Classic mode is untouched.

The test

Hypothesis: if score competitors get a friend's run to beat, more of today's players come back tomorrow, because something is finally waiting for them.

Setup90/10 holdout, 4 weeks; read weeks 3–4
PrimaryDAU
LeadingShare of today's players back tomorrow · expect ~+2 pts
Why it movedBattles per battler, revenge rate
GuardrailsVeterans' return in classic, ad revenue per DAU, crashes

Why not 50/50: a split would halve the pool of friends to battle. Battles match only inside the 90%; challenge links that reach the holdout are tagged and left out.

How it could fail

  • Novelty fades. Battles spike, then drop. That's why we read weeks 3–4, not week 1.
  • Losers quit. Watch return after a loss; match players to similar friends' runs and fall back to a bot.
Kill signalNext-day return up less than 1 point in weeks 3–4: stop and rethink.

Gameberry fit: ~95% of its growth was organic, with invites built into the core game (2021). Here, the invite is the match.

1010!'s players have nothing to come back for tomorrow. Block Battle turns every game over into a rival and a rematch, and the holdout tells us in 4 weeks.
